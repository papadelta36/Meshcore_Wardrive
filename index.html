        1. Overview & Purpose

        The Meshcore Signaal Meter & Wardrive app is designed for monitoring, logging, mapping, and communicating over Meshcore LoRa mesh networks via a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) companion repeater node. It provides real-time signal metrics, GPS wardriving logs, tactical CRT radar, and instant channel/direct messaging.

        2. Getting Started & Controls

        • Bluetooth Pairing: Open System Hub (≡) and tap "📶 Scan & Connect BLE" to select your Meshcore companion node.
        • Handshake & Sync: Upon connection, the app automatically executes the official handshake (Query ➔ App Start ➔ Channel Sync ➔ Contacts).
        • Fullscreen Toggle: Use the ⛶ button in the top bar to switch between Fullscreen and Standard Windowed mode according to user preference.
        • Persistent Storage: The app automatically requests Persistent Storage on startup to protect saved trips, contacts, and chat history against browser cache clearing.

        3. Dashboard & Radio Metrics

        • Speed & Trip Stats: Auto-scaling high-visibility GPS speed (KM/H) supporting 3-digit values, total trip distance (km), and duration.
        • Auto-Discover Progress: Aligned TIME (1m) and DIST (100m) progress bars at the bottom of the dashboard card.
        • VU Meters: Real-time RSSI (dBm) and SNR (dB) signal meters.
        • Tactical Radar & Location Marker: Interactive radar blips showing newly heard nodes and a clean glowing cyan position dot on the map.

        4. Filter Hub & Target Focus

        Tap "🔍 FILTERS" to filter displayed data on map and lists (all raw signals are always 100% saved in CSV logs):

        • Target Focus IDs: Focus exclusively on specific node IDs (e.g. 692F, 2F3A).
        • Traffic Types: Filter by Adverts (0x04), Direct Messages (0x02), Channels (0x05), Discover (0x0E), or RF Logs (0x88).
        • Zero Hop Only: Show only direct 0-hop signals from the transmitter.

        5. Maps & Repeater Positioning

        • Map Sources: Choose between OpenFreeMap Vector maps (Light/Dark HD) or Esri Gray raster maps.
        • Heatmap & Repeaters: Toggle coverage heatmap (🔥) and cyan repeater icons (🗼).
        • Trilateration: In Filter Hub, enter a target ID and tap "🎯 ESTIMATE REPEATER POSITION" to compute weighted RSSI repeater coordinates from historical trip data.

        6. Signal Deep Dive & Trajectories

        Long-press any signal log or contact to open Signal Deep Dive. Displays decoded raw hex, payload details, and tap "Full Repeater Path" to draw the complete hop-by-hop route on the map with flow arrows.

        7. Messenger, Scopes & Alerts

        • Channels & DMs: Switch between public/regional channels or private direct conversations.
        • Region Scope Badges: Decodes MeshCore Region Scopes (e.g. [#vlaanderen], [#be], [#nl], [#public]) using SHA-256 keys and HMAC-SHA256 transport codes.
        • In-App Map Alerts: Incoming messages trigger an in-app overlay banner exclusively when viewing the main Map UI ([MAP] tab). While in the Messenger ([CHAT] tab), messages appear quietly in real-time in the chat feed without overlay popups.
        • Driving Mode: When speed exceeds 5 km/h (or toggled manually), the messenger automatically expands into large, high-accessibility typography for safe in-car viewing.

        8. Data Export & Trips

        Tap "TRIPS" to export or import recorded sessions as CSV (with 9 standard columns and mesh_log_ naming prefix for 100% Android app compatibility) or KML (for Google Earth tracking).
