        1. Overview & Purpose

        The Meshcore Signaal Meter & Wardrive app is designed for monitoring, logging, mapping, and communicating over Meshcore LoRa mesh networks via a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) companion repeater node. It provides real-time signal metrics, GPS wardriving logs, tactical radar, and instant channel/direct messaging.

        2. Getting Started

        • Bluetooth Pairing: Open System Hub (≡) and tap "📶 Scan & Connect BLE" to select your Meshcore companion node.
        • Handshake & Sync: Upon connection, the app automatically executes the official handshake (Query ➔ App Start ➔ Channel Sync ➔ Contacts).
        • Start Trip: Tap "START" on the dashboard to record unfiltered GPS signal logs.

        3. Dashboard & Radio Metrics

        • Speed & Trip Stats: Live GPS speed (KM/H), total trip distance (km), and duration.
        • VU Meters: Real-time RSSI (dBm) and SNR (dB) signal meters.
        • Tactical Radar & Compass: 1:1 compass arrow indicating travel direction and interactive radar blips showing newly heard nodes. Tap the radar to open full-screen Tactical CRT Radar view.

        4. Filter Hub & Target Focus

        Tap "🔍 FILTERS" to filter displayed data on map and lists (all raw signals are always 100% saved in CSV logs):

        • Target Focus IDs: Focus exclusively on specific node IDs (e.g. 692F, 2F3A).
        • Traffic Types: Filter by Adverts (0x04), Direct Messages (0x02), Channels (0x05), Discover (0x0E), or RF Logs (0x88).
        • Zero Hop Only: Show only direct 0-hop signals from the transmitter.

        5. Maps & Repeater Positioning

        • Map Sources: Choose between OpenFreeMap Vector maps (Light/Dark HD) or Esri Gray raster maps.
        • Heatmap & Repeaters: Toggle coverage heatmap (🔥) and cyan repeater icons (🗼).
        • Trilateration: In Filter Hub, enter a target ID and tap "🎯 ESTIMATE REPEATER POSITION" to compute weighted RSSI repeater coordinates from historical trip data.

        6. Signal Deep Dive & Trajectories

        Long-press any signal log or contact to open Signal Deep Dive. Tap "Full Repeater Path" to draw the complete hop-by-hop route on the map with arrows and numbered hops.

        7. Messenger & Driving Mode (> 5 km/h)

        • Channels & DMs: Switch between public/regional channels or private direct conversations.
        • Hands-Free Voice Dictation: Tap 🎙️ to dictate messages with an automated 5-second send timer.
        • Driving Mode: When speed exceeds 5 km/h (or toggled manually), the messenger automatically expands into large, high-accessibility typography (`2.85rem` message text) for safe in-car viewing.
        • Delivery Status: Single checkmark (`✔`) indicates sent to radio; double green checkmark (`✔✔`) confirms network ACK delivery.

        8. Data Export & Trips

        Tap "TRIPS" to export recorded sessions as CSV (for Excel/Python analysis) or KML (for Google Earth visual tracking).
